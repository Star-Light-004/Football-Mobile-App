## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the vigor, sharpness, and precision of illuminated natural turf pitches paired with modern mobile-first digital booking. Tailored for athletes, team managers, and sports venue administrators, the aesthetic merges the reliability of enterprise utility with the kinetic vitality of competitive play. 

The visual language follows **Clean Emerald Modernism**:
- **Atmosphere**: Crisp, refreshing, and high-trust. Dominated by lush pitch greens set against cool slate foundations.
- **Hierarchy**: High clarity, minimal clutter, and tactical chunking designed for frictionless single-thumb interactions during on-the-go slot reservations.
- **Physical Motifs**: Subtle athletic skeuomorphism—incorporating perforated digital match tickets, high-contrast score-style tags, and tactical grid micro-interactions—while preserving flat, contemporary surface structures.

## Colors

The palette balances athletic emerald vibrancy with institutional slate stability:
- **Primary (`#16a34a`)**: Primary actions, selected stadium timeslots, and affirmative interaction states.
- **Secondary (`#15803d`) & Emerald Deep (`#055b35`)**: Anchor tones for headers, structured invoice badges, and prominent match receipts.
- **Tertiary (`#22c55e`)**: Live glowing indicators, active focus borders, and interactive accents.
- **Neutral (`#f8fafc`)**: Neutral base surface preventing mobile glare under sunlight, contrasted with pure white cards (`#ffffff`) and slate dark modules (`#0f172a`) for console modes.
- **Domain Status Spectrum**: Granular sports management statuses (Prime hours, pitch cleaning, maintenance, lockouts) use tailored low-saturation backgrounds paired with deep border and text pairings to satisfy WCAG AA contrast.

## Typography

Typography centers exclusively on **Be Vietnam Pro** for its crisp rendering of Vietnamese diacritics, geometric balance, and high structural legibility on iOS high-DPI displays.

- **Tabular Figures**: All currency, VietQR transaction identifiers, clock schedules, and remaining time counts must activate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter during real-time synchronization.
- **Hierarchy & Case**: Headlines leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) for athletic assertiveness. Labels, status chips, and metadata use semi-bold weights with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scannability on small screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a strict 4px/8px structural increment adapted for fluid mobile responsiveness:

- **Mobile Viewport (<768px)**: Single column with a fixed `16px` outer margin. Critical workflows (booking checkout, VietQR payment) dock actions to a sticky bottom sheet (`z-index: 100`) providing 48px+ touch targets.
- **Tablet / Split View (768px–1024px)**: Dual-pane layout—stadium inventory on the left, sticky booking ledger on the right. Margins step up to `24px`.
- **Desktop Management (>1024px)**: 12-column layout with a fixed enterprise navigation rail, bounded to max `1200px` for booking portals and 100% fluid width with `32px` gutters for stadium dispatch consoles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean chromatic layering and soft, daylight-inspired ambient glows:

- **Surface Base**: `#f8fafc` serves as ground zero; elevated modules utilize pure `#ffffff`.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Base Card)**: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` with a hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). Applied to field directory cards and schedule grids.
- **Level 2 (Active Modules & Overlays)**: `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`. Applied to interactive cards, date scrollers, and floating bottom taskbars.
- **Level 3 (Primary Glow / Selection)**: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.25), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.20)`. Exclusively accentuates selected time slots and the primary conversion CTA.
- **Level 4 (Sheets & Modals)**: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18)` positioned above backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system applies a calibrated rounded aesthetic (Level 2 base):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Buttons, Time Slots)**: `8px - 10px` for ergonomic thumb alignment and crisp boundaries.
- **Standard Cards (`FieldCard`, Summary Sheets)**: `14px - 16px` (`rounded-lg`) offering modern softness without wasting screen estate.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers**: `20px - 24px` (`rounded-xl`) at top corners for mobile sheet presentations.
- **Chips, Category Selectors, Pill CTAs**: `9999px` (Full round) to reinforce athletic agility and quick-action touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill or 10px radius with `#16a34a` fill, white bold text, and Level 3 emerald glow (`rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.25)`). Transitions to `translateY(-2px)` on touch/hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White surface with `1px solid #e2e8f0` border, `#111827` text; activates `#f0fdf4` tint with `#16a34a` text on press.
- **Disabled**: `#f1f5f9` fill, `#94a3b8` text, no shadow.

### Timeslot Selector Tiles
- **Available**: White surface, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, dark text, green subtle hover highlight.
- **Selected**: `#f0fdf4` surface with a high-contrast `2px solid #16a34a` border and `#15803d` bold text.
- **Booked / Locked**: `#f1f5f9` surface, `#94a3b8` muted text, with a centered miniature lock icon; non-interactive.
- **Prime Hour Accent**: Tagged with a top-right flame icon badge in amber tones (`#b45309`).

### Digital Match Pass (VietQR Ticket)
- **Top Stub**: Stadium hero photo, field designation, match time, and countdown.
- **Perforated Divider**: A dashed horizontal separation line flanked by semi-circular punch-out notches on left and right borders simulating a tear-off stadium pass.
- **Bottom Stub (Payment & Entry)**: Centered high-resolution dynamic VietQR pass, numeric booking code with copy icon, and bank settlement validation badge.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Built with a `9999px` pill radius, `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Uses semantic pairs: Success (`#dcfce7`/`#15803d`), Prime (`#fef3c7`/`#b45309`), Danger (`#fee2e2`/`#b91c1c`), Maintenance (`#ffedd5`/`#c2410c`), Pitch Cleaning (`#ccfbf1`/`#0f766e`).
- Live ongoing matches feature an animated pulsing dot (`tertiary_color_hex` `#22c55e`).

### Form Inputs & Search
- Surface: `#ffffff` with `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `10px` border-radius, `12px 16px` padding.
- Focused state: Hairline transition to `#16a34a` outline and `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Stadium Modules
- Bounded inside `14px - 16px` radii on pure white surfaces. Image containers use a fixed 16:9 ratio with gradient scrim overlays for title legibility.
